10 / THREE CONNECTED IDEAS, ONE VISUAL

Three parts of a complete explanation.

01

Describe the context.

Introduce the example and its main elements. Give the reader a clear starting point before adding detail.

02

Explain the relationship.

Explain how the elements connect. Use consistent names so the reader can follow the same part in the visual.

03

Make it reviewable.

Connect the explanation to visible observations. Finish by checking the relationship against the complete picture.

Additional context can explain an unfamiliar term, describe another example or connect this topic to an earlier lesson. Keep the wording visible at the same reading size when checking whether the chosen layout still has enough space.
