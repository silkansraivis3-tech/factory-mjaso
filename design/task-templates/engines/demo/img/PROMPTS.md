Photorealistic photograph, 16:9, of the cargo control room of a modern LPG gas carrier. A wide, clean
grey console runs across the frame at desk height, with switches, a telephone and a few small gauges.

but showing a plain very dark navy screen with nothing on it. Behind the console, large windows look
out over the cargo deck: piping, tank domes, a blue sky with soft daylight. Calm, professional, soft
cool-blue interior light, shallow depth of field on the far deck, no people, no text, no logos.

Photorealistic photograph, 16:9, of the cargo manifold on the deck of an LPG carrier during
discharge in daylight. Liquid and vapour lines with large flanged valves and hand wheels, a
connected loading arm or hose to shore, drip tray under the connections. Two crew members in
orange coveralls, white helmets and face shields stand at a safe distance. Clean, realistic, slightly
warm evening light, no text, no logos.

realistic, no people, no text, no logos.
