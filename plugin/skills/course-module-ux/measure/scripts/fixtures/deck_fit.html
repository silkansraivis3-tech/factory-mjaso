Why does the fully pressurised ship stay small?

Pressure alone keeps the cargo liquid, so the tank walls must be thick: heavy tanks, poor use of the hull, fewer tonnes carried. Cooled cargo has a lower vapour pressure: thinner, lighter, cheaper tanks and more cargo for the same tank size - paid for with insulation and a reliquefaction plant.
