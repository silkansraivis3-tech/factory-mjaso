Ethylene (LEG) carriers

Ethylene carriers look much like semi-pressurised ships; the main difference is a design temperature of -104 C.

  • Independent Type C tanks of nickel steel or stainless steel need no secondary barrier and have thicker insulation.
  • A cascade reliquefaction plant keeps the ethylene fully refrigerated.
  • Sizes are typically 2,000-12,000 m3.
