NOVIKONTAS course factory · figure kit

Ready-made parts that draw themselves from a short spec and move between steps. The AI writes the spec, and the kit does the drawing, the motion and the alarms. Every alarm fires at the moment its value crosses the mark, so the order of events is always right. Seven families are planned; two are built.

2 · Piping and flow

  
Pipes know what is in them and how far it has reached: liquid is cold blue, and gas is pale
    (methane vapour has no colour, and amber stays reserved for alarms). The fluid front moves along the pipe,
    valves close it off, and a pipe can empty towards a point, like the bleed between two block valves.


  
Slide s03 - the LNG fuel system, tank to engine

  
The five steps from the slide text. Each step lights its space and badge, and the fuel moves on
    one stage at a time.

  


  
Slide s04 - gas valve unit, double block and bleed

  
On a shutdown the two block valves close (fail-to-close) and the engine stops.
    Then the bleed valve opens (fail-to-open), and the gas trapped between the block valves vents to the open air.
