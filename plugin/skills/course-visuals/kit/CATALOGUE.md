# Figure kit catalogue

A spec places parts, never their words (the kit puts every label where it is clear). Draw to the panel:
`size` has the shape of the panel - two-col about 640x390, dark about 680x415 - and the drawing covers it.
Full reference: SPEC.md (formats, rules), PARTS.md (making a new part). Check: `check_kit.py spec <file>`.

## Spec
`{"size":[w,h], "label":"one sentence for the screen reader", "idle":"text at step 0", "parts":[...], "steps":[...]}`
Also: `view:[x,y,w,h]` (task pages), `theme:"light"|"dark"`, `fit:false` (never zoom to fill), `clock:"10:42:00"`.
Every part: `id` (unique), `type`, `x`, `y`, `value` (its starting value), plus its options below.

## Relations - place by what a part stands on, never a guessed y (SPEC.md "Relations")
`"on":"G.deck"` (vent mast, manifold, hatch) · `"in":"Hold"` · `"at":0.7|"K.top"|"P1@0.4"` (ON a pipe) · pipe/hose
`"from":"T.top","to":"V.inlet"` · text `"for":"B"` · zone `"around":"V.outlet"` · cloud `"from":"V.outlet"` · spray
`"aim":"W"` · ga `"cut":true`. A figure that MEANS something wrong fails the build. Start from `patterns/` when one fits.

## Marks (on level, gauge, readout: `marks:[...]`)
`{"at":98, "label":"shut-off", "kind":"alarm|trip|limit", "dir":"up|down", "stop":true,
 "alarm":"text for the log and chip", "then":[["V1",0,700]]}`
alarm amber, trip red, limit grey; quiet until crossed; `dir` up (default) fires rising through `at`; `stop` holds the
value at the mark; `then` moves to start the moment it is crossed; marks fire in the order the value meets them.

## Steps (`steps:[...]`)
`{"title":"1 · Bunkering", "reset":true, "set":{"F1":1}, "play":[["L1",100,5200,300,"linear"]], "line":2}`
- `play` item = `[partId, to, ms, delay, ease]`; ease `inout` (default) | `linear` | `out`. Second value of a part:
  `"P1.fill"`, `"T1.burst"`, `"C1.drift"` (see the parts).
- `reset` back to the starting values and clear alarms (independent scenarios); `set` jumps values without crossing marks.
- `line` (number or list) lights the slide's own text line(s): `.cur` this step, `.lit` the earlier; a lit line is a tap target.
- Step n starts from the end of step n-1; moves on one part queue up; Previous and Replay are the deck's.
